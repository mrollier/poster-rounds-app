# Ghent places for posters and flyers: research (26 November 2026)

Four research passes (books & ideas, music, animal welfare & sustainability, community & cafés) using the places' own websites, stad.gent, visit.gent.be, data.stad.gent and OpenStreetMap (coordinates from Nominatim). Every place was checked for signs of life in 2025–2026. Result: **13 new types of place and 191 places** (185 active, 6 inactive with low confidence), on top of the 198 existing ones.

**Ghent forbids posters outside the city boards**, so every new place is "ask first". The tip in each place's notes says whom to ask and when.

## Things to know

- **Left out because closed or not a public spot:** Pink Flamingo's (closed for good), Simon Says, The English Bookshop (closed 2018), Vredeshuis, Opa Tuur, ZoeZoe Records, Kompass Klub (techno club), De Meubelfabriek, Vrijwilligerspunt Gent (closing after city cuts), Velt/Climaxi/GAIA/Animal Rights (no public spot in Ghent), Think Twice and vintage boutiques.
- **Buurtinfokasten** (free neighbourhood poster cases) are not added: the city only allows posters for activities in that same neighbourhood. The city publishes a PDF list of them (version 27 January 2026) if you want them anyway.
- **Offices rather than shops** (better to email first; they may share the event in their own channels): ProVeg Belgium (Steendam 84), Gents MilieuFront, Masereelfonds.
- **Limited opening hours:** DreamCATchers (Wed and Sat), Plantbaar (Wed–Sat 11–18), Pacha Mama (lunch only), Dierenasiel Gent (afternoons 13:30–16:00). See the notes.
- **One place outside Ghent:** VOC Merelbeke (wildlife rescue), handy with a round to the Merelbeke vet campus.
- **For AI Safety:** tech companies' offices aren't public, so none are added. The AI Tinkerers Ghent meetup (February 2026) was held at Aikido Security, Keizer Karelstraat 15, if you want to approach them directly.
- **Café notice boards are assumed, not confirmed:** no café states a flyer policy publicly.
- **Duplicates removed:** 8 places found by two passes are merged (De Centrale, Hotsy Totsy, Rokko, Asgaard, Maegher Goet, Broei, KAPOW, De Loge).
